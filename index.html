About

A steady hand for the numbers behind your business.

I'm Krysha Ann Uraga, a bookkeeping and accounting specialist supporting startups, entrepreneurs, freelancers and small business owners who need their finances handled properly — not squeezed in between other tasks.

My work is built on accuracy and organization: transactions recorded consistently, accounts reconciled, records structured so they can be trusted, reviewed, and handed to a lender, auditor, or tax professional without apology. I treat every client's financial data with the confidentiality it deserves, and I communicate in plain language so you always understand what your numbers are saying.

Whether you're catching up on overdue books, preparing for a busy season, or simply want a dependable professional keeping your records current, my role is to make that part of your business quiet, orderly, and off your mind.
